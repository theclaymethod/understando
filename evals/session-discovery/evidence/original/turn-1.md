# Recording playback after the owner disables its public address

**Coverage is partial.** Runner r18 verified members-only playback. No recorded run tried a fresh public play after disablement, and r17 observed an active stream stop. Its PASS reflects the developer’s superseded expectation. The largest access-control gap is whether someone who listened before disablement can play the recording again.

| Owner decision in [session.txt](session.txt) | Recorded check and observation | Check needed and coverage judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A fresh public play after disablement must be refused, including a repeat listen. | r18 `public page/playerVisibility` passed when the Play control disappeared. It made no fresh playback attempt. | **Proposed, unverified.** Play the recording through its public address before disablement and retain that player session. After the owner disables the address, initiate a fresh playback request through the retained session. Repeat with a first-time public listener. For each attempt, require refusal and verify that no playable audio is delivered. Hiding the control alone cannot pass this check. |
| A public stream already playing when the owner disables the address can finish. The developer explicitly corrected the earlier immediate-interruption rule. | r17 `public page/interruptActive` heard five seconds before disablement and received no further audio bytes afterward. The old check passed on the outcome the corrected rule should reject. | **Proposed replacement, awaiting replay.** Use a recording with audio still to be delivered after five seconds. Disable its public address while those first seconds are playing, then let the same play continue. Require audible playback through the end. An immediate cutoff fails. Starting another play after completion exercises the fresh-play rule above. |
| A signed-in group member can still play the recording on the members-only page. | r18 `private page/memberPlayback` passed with the public address disabled. The member played the full recording on that page. | **Covered under the stated conditions.** Retain the existing full-playback check. |

**Open boundary.** The engineer asked about a public playback request made before disablement that has received zero audio bytes at the switch. The developer explicitly deferred that decision. Give this case no pass/fail expectation. Keep the fresh-play check’s request after the switch and the active-stream check’s audible start before it.
